# 🍽️ Meal Planner

🔗 **Live App:** [meal-planner-hub.vercel.app](https://meal-planner-hub.vercel.app/)

A simple web app that helps families decide what to cook every day — based on what they actually love to eat.

## The Problem

Every day, families face the same question: *"What should we cook today?"* Even when someone finally decides, there's a good chance the food won't be liked by everyone, and it ends up wasted. Deciding what to cook — something everyone will actually enjoy — is harder than it sounds.

## The Solution

Meal Planner lets you save the dishes your family already loves, along with their main ingredients and how much everyone liked them. Based on that, the app suggests a dish to cook today — prioritizing meals you haven't cooked in a while, and the ones your family loves the most.

## Features

- **Add your own dishes** — save dish names, main ingredients, and a rating (Loved / Okay / Disliked)
- **Smart daily suggestion** — the app picks what to cook today based on your ratings and how recently each dish was cooked
- **Similar dish suggestions** — see other saved dishes that share ingredients with today's pick
- **Mark as cooked** — track when a dish was last made, so the app doesn't repeat it too soon
- **Edit and delete dishes** — update or remove any saved dish anytime
- **Discover new recipes** — get real recipe ideas based on ingredients you already cook with, complete with full instructions
- **Supports local ingredient names** — understands common Pakistani/Urdu ingredient names (like *aloo*, *gobi*, *daal*) and matches them to real recipes
- **User accounts** — each person has their own private list of dishes, with secure login
- **Light and dark mode** — smooth, modern interface that adapts to your preference
- **Works on mobile and desktop**

## Technology Used

- **React** (with Vite) — for building the interface
- **Tailwind CSS** — for styling and design
- **Firebase** — Authentication (user accounts) and Firestore (database)
- **TheMealDB API** — for real recipe data and instructions
- **Lucide Icons** — for the visual icons throughout the app
